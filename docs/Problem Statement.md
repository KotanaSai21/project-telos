## Problem Statement
Build an AI-powered solution that transforms how content is created, discovered, personalized, understood, or experienced. Solutions may address challenges across media, entertainment, publishing, education, communication, or other digital content ecosystems.

## Suggested Solution Directions
Personalized content recommendations, interactive storytelling, content creation, misinformation analysis, audience insights, sentiment analysis, personalized learning

## Suggested Google Cloud Technologies
Gemini on Vertex AI, Imagen for image generation, Veo for video generation, Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech for audio experiences, Cloud Translation for multilingual experiences, BigQuery for audience analytics, Cloud Storage for media assets, Cloud Run for content applications and APIs

## Judging criteria (Submissions are scored against four weighted criteria):
1. Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation (40%)
    - <b>Meaningful use of Gen AI</b>: How effectively and meaningfully has Generative AI been incorporated into the solution? Does the use of Gen AI contribute directly to solving the problem or enhancing the overall solution?
    - <b>Technical implementation</b>: Is the solution functional, technically sound, and appropriately implemented?
    - <b>Scalability & sustainability</b>: Does the solution demonstrate potential for wider adoption, scalability, and future development beyond the prototype stage?
2. Problem Alignment & Impact (25%)
    - <b>Problem alignment</b>: How effectively does the solution address the chosen problem statement, challenge objective, or theme?
    - <b>Potential impact</b>: Does the solution demonstrate clear and meaningful value for its intended users, organization, industry, community, or other relevant stakeholders?
3. Innovation & Creativity (25%)
    - <b>Originality & differentiation</b>: Does the solution demonstrate a fresh perspective, novel approach, or meaningful differentiation from existing solutions?
    - <b>Innovation in the use of Gen AI</b>: Does the solution demonstrate a creative or innovative application of Generative AI beyond a basic or superficial implementation?
4. User Experience & Solution Design (10%)
    - <b>Usability & accessibility</b>: Is the solution intuitive, user-friendly, and reasonably accessible to its intended users?
    - <b>Experience & integration</b>: Do the various components, including the Gen AI capabilities, work together cohesively to deliver a smooth and meaningful user experience?

## What you'll submit
Following requirements are mandatory while submitting your solution:
1.  Clear Proposal:
    - Submit a compelling vision for your AI solution, highlighting its positive impact and alignment with one of the problem statements in a deck/PPT converted into a PDF.
    - Clearly articulate the problem your solution addresses and how it will benefit the community.
    - Demonstrate the feasibility of your project and its potential for scalability.
2. Functional Prototype:
    - Build a working prototype using Google Cloud's AI tools & deployed on Cloud Run/GCP/Firebase.
    - Ensure the prototype effectively demonstrates your solution's capabilities and benefits.
3. Comprehensive Documentation:
    - Include a link to a public YouTube or Vimeo video or public Google drive link (3 minutes) showcasing your AI creation in action.
4. Category Specification:
    - Clearly identify the problem statement you are submitting for (Healthcare, Education, Sustainability, Accessibility, Social Good).

## Submission Specifics
1. Functionality
    - Your project must be functional and demonstrate a clear solution to the chosen social challenge.
    - The solution should be user-friendly, accessible, and scalable.
    - Ensure that your project aligns with the hackathon's focus on social impact.

    - Your solution should be compatible with the specified Google Cloud platform and tools.
    - Consider factors like scalability, performance, and integration with existing systems.
3. Language
    - All submission materials, including code, documentation, and presentations, must be in English.

## Submission Instruction (Mandatory Submission Rules):

1. <b>Pitch Deck</b>: Participants must submit their pitch deck using the prescribed template provided for the challenge. Submissions that do not follow the required format may be subject to disqualification
2. <b>Video Demonstration</b>: Participants must submit a 3 to 4 minute video demonstration of their solution. The video must clearly demonstrate the working functionality of the submitted solution. Videos exceeding the prescribed duration may attract a penalty or may result in the submission not progressing further in the evaluation process. If the video does not adequately demonstrate the working functionality of the solution, the submission will not progress to the prototype evaluation step, in accordance with the defined ladder process.
3. <b>Deployed Prototype Link</b>: Participants must provide a valid and accessible link to a deployed, working prototype. The prototype must remain functional throughout the evaluation process. If the application, APIs, integrations, or any other deployment-related components are unavailable or non-functional during evaluation, no additional consideration, retries, or allowances will be provided. The submission will be evaluated based on its functionality at the time of evaluation.
4. <b>GitHub Repository</b>: Participants must provide an accessible GitHub repository containing the relevant source code and supporting materials. The repository must remain accessible throughout the evaluation process.
5. <b>Brief description</b>: Brief description of what you have built (please include how you are leveraging Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Run and Gemini in your submission).
